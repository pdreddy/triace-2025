How teams are placed
  • When registering, a team can state a tier preference or choose “Interested in a higher UTR tier.”
  • A team is placed in the highest active tier whose range includes its combined UTR, even if it preferred a lower tier.
  • If tier ranges overlap, the admin's tier order decides placement.
  • A team whose combined UTR is outside every active tier can't register.
